About

I find companies with more inside them than their results admit.

The story

I’m Alex Birkett. I’ve spent my career inside growth teams, building the thing rather than advising from the side.

I doubled blog traffic at CXL and helped launch CXL Institute. I built growth experiments and custom data products at HubSpot, including a SERP analysis tool for brand mentions and a model that predicted content decay. I built Workato’s experimentation program from scratch to more than 20 experiments a quarter. I helped grow Jasper’s blog into a program with more than $4M in attributable ARR. I co-founded an organic growth agency and scaled it past 35 people.

Across all of it, the same pattern kept showing up. The companies that pulled away weren’t the ones following the playbook most faithfully. They were the ones running experiments the playbook hadn’t caught up to yet, and building teams that could keep doing it.

Now AI search has scrambled how buyers discover, trust and choose companies. Nobody has the final map. Not the platforms, not the tool vendors, not me. What I do have is a method: measure honestly, test fast, keep what works, and build that habit into the team so it outlasts any single engagement.

That’s Stella Nova.

Why “Stella Nova”

A nova is a star that suddenly burns far brighter than it did before. That’s the job: find the company with more inside it than it’s showing, and help it become the N of 1 in its category.

What I believe
