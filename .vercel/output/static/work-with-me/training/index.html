The short version

Most AI-search training is a slide deck of screenshots and a list of tips that expire in a month. This isn’t that.

Every session is built around your companies, your categories, and the questions your buyers are actually typing into ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google. We look at real answers, take apart why some brands get named and others don’t, and leave with experiments your team owns.

Who it’s for

Investors and their portfolios

Run one program across your portfolio so every marketing team learns the new rules at the same time, and the operating partners get a shared language for what good looks like.

In-house marketing teams

SEO, content, PR and product marketing teams who need to stop guessing and start running a coordinated organic program built for answer engines.

Leadership teams

CEOs, CMOs and boards deciding where to place bets. A clear-eyed briefing on what’s changing, what’s hype, and what to fund.

What’s included

The work.

  • How answer engines choose

    How large language models and AI search surfaces find, weigh and cite sources, and what that means for which brands get recommended.

  • Measuring without fooling yourself

    How to track AI visibility honestly: prompt sets, sampling, share of voice, and why most dashboards overstate what they know.

  • Earning the citation

    What gets pages, research and third-party coverage cited, and how to build a program around it.

  • The experimentation habit

    How to design, run and read organic experiments so your team learns faster than the market changes.

  • From visibility to revenue

    Tying AI search to pipeline and revenue, so the program survives its first budget review.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    We agree on audience, goals and format. I review your sites and categories before we meet.

  2. 02

    Custom build

    I pull real AI answers for your categories so the session runs on your data, not generic examples.

  3. 03

    The session

    Teaching, live teardown, and working time. Every team builds its own prioritized experiment list.

  4. 04

    Follow-through

    Materials, the recording, and a written summary of each team’s next moves.

You leave with

  1. A shared, accurate model of how AI search works

  2. A prioritized list of experiments each team owns

  3. Session materials and the recording

  4. A written readout for leadership or operating partners

Questions

Remote or in person?

Either. Most half-day sessions run remotely; full-day workshops and portfolio offsites work best in person.

Can you run this across multiple portfolio companies at once?

Yes. That’s one of the most common formats. Companies learn together, then break out to work on their own categories.

Is this technical?

It’s built for marketers and operators, not engineers. Technical topics come up where they matter, explained in plain language.
