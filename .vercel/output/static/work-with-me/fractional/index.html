The short version

Some companies don’t need advice. They need someone to lead: set the strategy, run the team and agencies, build the operating system, and report to the board on what it’s returning.

I take this on for two companies at a time, total. That limit is the product. It’s how I stay close enough to the work to actually move it.

Who it’s for

Companies between leaders

You need senior organic and AI search leadership now, and the right full-time hire is months away.

Portfolio companies at an inflection point

A value-creation plan depends on organic growth and there’s no time for a learning curve.

Brands making a category bet

You intend to be the name AI answers give in your category, and you want someone accountable for getting there.

What’s included

The work.

  • Strategy and roadmap ownership

    I own the organic and AI search strategy, the roadmap, and the trade-offs.

  • Team and agency leadership

    Lead your in-house team and manage agencies and freelancers against clear standards.

  • An experimentation operating system

    Build the cadence, tooling and rituals that turn experimentation into a habit, not a project.

  • Measurement and reporting

    AI visibility, organic pipeline and revenue reporting that leadership and the board actually trust.

  • Hire your successor

    Recruit, onboard and hand off to your permanent leader when the time is right.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Fit conversation

    With only two seats, we both need to be sure. We talk about goals, team and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    First 30 days

    Audit, team assessment, and a 90-day plan with clear targets.

  3. 03

    Operate

    Weekly leadership, monthly reporting, quarterly resets. I run the program alongside your team.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Build the team and system to the point where it runs without me.

You leave with

  1. An organic and AI search program that compounds

  2. A team that knows how to run it

  3. Reporting the board trusts

  4. A permanent leader ready to take it forward

Because this is real leadership, not a retainer with a bigger title. Two is the number where I can stay close enough to the work to own the outcome.

Enough to lead: regular presence with the team, weekly leadership involvement, and availability for the decisions that matter.
