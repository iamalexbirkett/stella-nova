The short version

You can’t prioritize what you haven’t measured. Most companies know their keyword rankings to the decimal and have no idea whether ChatGPT recommends them, a competitor, or nobody at all.

An audit gives you an honest baseline, a clear view of the gaps, and a roadmap ranked by impact, so the next quarter goes to the work that moves the number.

Who it’s for

Brands

Teams that want a defensible plan for AI search before they hire, reallocate budget, or sign another agency.

Investors

A clear read on a company’s organic position for diligence, the first 100 days, or a value-creation plan.

What’s included

The work.

  • AI visibility baseline

    Where you show up, and how you’re described, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google’s AI surfaces for the prompts your buyers actually use.

  • Competitive gap analysis

    Who gets named instead of you, for which questions, and why.

  • Source landscape

    The publications, reviews, communities and pages that shape the answers in your category, and where you’re missing.

  • Site readiness

    Whether AI crawlers can reach, parse and trust your site: technical access, structure, and how clearly your company is defined.

  • Organic search health

    Traditional search still feeds the answer engines. Where you’re strong, where you’re leaking, and what’s at risk.

  • Prioritized roadmap

    Every recommendation ranked by impact and effort, with owners and the experiment that proves it worked.

How it runs

  1. 01

    Kickoff

    Goals, competitors, the questions that matter to your buyers, and access to analytics and search data.

  2. 02

    Research

    Prompt sampling across AI platforms, competitive and source analysis, technical and organic review.

  3. 03

    Readout

    A working session with your team and leadership. Findings first, then the plan.

  4. 04

    Roadmap handoff

    The full report and a roadmap your team can start on Monday.

You leave with

  1. An honest AI visibility baseline you can re-measure

  2. A clear view of who’s winning your answers and why

  3. A ranked roadmap with owners and experiments

  4. A leadership-ready readout
