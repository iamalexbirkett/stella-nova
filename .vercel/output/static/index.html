The thing nobody says out loud

Nobody has the map yet. Including the people selling it.

Every week brings a new model, a new search surface, and a new dashboard promising your “AI share of voice.” Most of it is guesswork with a chart on top.

The companies that win this stretch won’t be the ones with the most confident vendor. They’ll be the ones that measure honestly, run real experiments, and move before the answer is obvious. That’s a muscle, not a project. I help teams build it.

  1. Being the answer matters more than ranking for the question.

  2. Best practices make you average. Experiments make you singular.

  3. If you can’t tie it to pipeline, it’s a hobby.

How I work

Eye level.
High ceiling.

Plain talk, no theater, and a bar set higher than you’d set it yourself. Then I stay in the room while your team clears it.

  1. Research before opinions.
